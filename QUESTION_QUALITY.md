# First question-quality pass

General culture (15), reasoning (15), and science (12) now have individually written answer explanations and four distinct choices. Science is labelled medium; reasoning uses conditional reasoning, probability, and explicit premises rather than elementary arithmetic labelled hard. This pass covers these three built-in banks, not the entire catalogue.

Source links are shown after answer confirmation where attached. Institutional references include UNESCO Göbekli Tepe, UK Parliament Magna Carta, Instituto Cervantes, Library of Congress, Vatican Museums, Royal Ballet and Opera, US Naval Observatory calendar rules, and NASA seasons, Moon phases, light-years and transits. Other mathematical and experimental-method questions explain their reasoning directly. These are editorial quizzes, not validated ability or IQ measurements.

Validation: tests/answer-feedback.cjs checks every refreshed item with correct and incorrect answers at mobile/desktop widths, plus scoring, resume, practice, mixed mode and speed timeout (346 checks). tests/question-reasoning.cjs independently enumerates box statements, seating, probability, team selection and age constraints (10 checks). No production accounts, answers or scores are created by these checks.

## Full catalog editorial pass — 14 November 2026

All 81 public knowledge banks (1,170 questions) now include answer explanations. Distractors were reviewed for topic and answer type; off-topic sections in the body, animal, art and automotive banks were replaced. Ambiguous conditions, units, alphabet, sport variants and exceptions are stated explicitly. Music now covers musical concepts instead of shallow celebrity trivia, and the puzzle bank uses explicit rules and reasoning. Unsupported expert labels were replaced with topic-based labels. Existing test IDs, history, daily challenge hooks and game data remain in place.

149 public tests/forms/games have separate static guide pages under /testler/. Knowledge pages include the complete explained review in expandable sections; creative and self-reflection pages state their limits and method. IPIP pages explicitly describe an unvalidated Turkish draft and provide source links. These pages do not load account SDKs, analytics or advertising. /testler/ links every guide, and the sitemap contains 155 URLs. The main catalog links each guide; /?quiz= uses an allowlisted catalog to open the existing engine. Monthly mystery keys and unpublished private bonus material are never included.

The scoped database migration updates only existing public knowledge questions/titles and their catalog descriptions. It checks for concurrent content edits, retains a pre-change backup and verifies readback. It does not modify users, memberships, votes, answers, scores, rules or ranked game content. Author seeds stay outside the published repository.

Validation: content-quality.cjs checks 81 banks/1,170 explained questions plus known ambiguity/off-topic regressions (5,973 checks with the release fixture); test-pages.cjs checks all 149 pages (1,865 checks); test-pages-browser.cjs checks mobile/desktop, nine engines, anonymous access and no completion writes (79 checks). Existing answer feedback (346), universal daily challenge (263), discovery (32), independent reasoning (10) and result insight logic (29) also pass. No approval or ranking outcome is promised; AdSense review is a separate Google decision.
